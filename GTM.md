# 🚀 Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

**Credex** aims to become the standard for "AI Capital Efficiency" in the startup ecosystem.

## 🎯 Target Audience
- **Primary**: CTOs and Heads of Operations at Series A-C startups.
- **Secondary**: Finance teams at mid-market enterprises managing "shadow IT" AI spend.
- **Tertiary**: VC Operating Partners looking to optimize their portfolio burn rates.

## 📈 Distribution Channels

### 1. The "Viral Audit" (Product-Led Growth)
The free Audit tool serves as the top-of-funnel (TOFU). Users run an audit, see "Potential Annual Savings," and are prompted to share a redacted version on X/LinkedIn.

### 2. VC Portfolio Partnerships
Partner with VC firms to offer Credex as a "Portfolio Burn Audit" service. VCs get a dashboard of portfolio efficiency; founders get 30% savings.

### 3. Engineering Influencers
Seed the tool with high-visibility engineering leaders on X/LinkedIn to normalize the "Audit your AI Stack" behavior.

## 💰 Monetization
- **Self-Service**: Free basic audit. $49/mo for real-time tracking and team-seat management.
- **Enterprise**: $299+/mo for custom redundancy rules, SSO, and multi-team dashboards.
- **Advisory**: Success-fee based model (e.g., 20% of first-year savings) for managed migrations.

## 🌊 Viral Hooks
- "Your AI Efficiency Score is 64%. How do you rank?"
- "We found $12k in annual waste in 4 minutes. Find yours."
- "The State of AI Spend 2026" (Annual industry report powered by anonymized Credex data).
