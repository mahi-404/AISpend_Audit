# Project Upgrade: AI Capital Optimization Platform

## Strategic Vision
The platform has been transitioned from a simple cost calculator to a comprehensive **AI Capital Optimization Engine**. The focus shifted from "saving money" to "strategic capital allocation" and "operational efficiency."

## Architectural Upgrades

### 1. Financial Intelligence Layer (`AuditEngine`)
- **Weighted Efficiency Scoring**: Beyond simple math, the engine now calculates a multi-factor efficiency score based on seat utilization, redundancy overlap, and market benchmarks.
- **Risk Analysis**: Detects "Tool Sprawl" and "Security Redundancy" where overlapping providers (e.g. ChatGPT + Claude) create unnecessary operational complexity.
- **Precision Financials**: Implemented strict rounding and yearly projections to eliminate floating-point perception issues in B2B SaaS billing.

### 2. High-Performance Design System (`globals.css`)
- **Institutional Aesthetics**: Implemented a "Dark Mode First" premium aesthetic using custom OKLCH color tokens, glassmorphism (`premium-blur`), and sophisticated grid backgrounds.
- **Dynamic Hierarchy**: Used high-impact typography (Black-weight headings, 0.9 line-height) and subtle micro-animations to drive user engagement and trust.
- **Component Polish**: Refactored shadcn/ui components to use custom gradients and glow effects, elevating the "Credex" brand identity.

### 3. Professional Audit Workflow
- **Multi-Phase Configuration**: The `AuditForm` now operates as a professional configuration tool with company profiling, capital allocation, and final validation steps.
- **Executive Reporting**: The `AuditDashboard` provides consultant-grade visualizations, including SVG gauges for benchmark percentiles and detailed "Strategic Intervention" cards with operational tradeoffs.
- **AI-Driven Strategy**: Claude 3.5 Sonnet now generates executive summaries focused on "capital allocation" and "strategic consolidation" rather than just "downgrades."

## Conversion & Trust Engineering
- **Institutional Trust Signals**: Added confidence ratings, market benchmark percentiles, and "Verified" data integrity badges.
- **Enterprise Lead Flow**: Implemented a sophisticated `LeadCaptureModal` to transition high-value users from a free tool into a professional advisory relationship.
- **Social Proofing**: Rebuilt the landing page with high-fidelity social proof and case study narratives to drive virality.

## Next Steps

- Expand market benchmarks with industry-specific data (e.g. Fintech vs. Healthtech AI spend).
- Add "Team Seat Right-Sizing" simulation tool.
