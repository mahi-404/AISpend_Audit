# 🗣 User Interviews & Discovery

Discovery sessions were conducted with 5 CTOs and 3 Operations Leads to validate the "AI Tool Sprawl" hypothesis.

## 🔑 Key Discovery Findings

1. **"Shadow IT" is Real**: 100% of interviewed CTOs suspected developers had personal "Plus" accounts they weren't reporting, or multiple teams were paying for the same API.
2. **The "Seat Trap"**: 4/5 startups were paying for "Team" plans (min 2-5 seats) for tools only used by a single champion.
3. **Redundancy Fatigue**: "I don't know if we should use ChatGPT or Claude. Half the team uses one, half uses the other. We're paying for both for everyone."
4. **Desire for Benchmarking**: "I want to know if spending $2k/mo on AI is normal for a 15-person team."

## 📝 Interview Personas

### Persona A: "The Thrifty CTO"
- **Goal**: Maintain 24 months of runway.
- **Pain**: Feeling "nickeled and dimed" by $20/mo subscriptions that add up to $3k/mo.
- **Value**: Immediate waste identification.

### Persona B: "The Operations Lead"
- **Goal**: Consolidate billing and security.
- **Pain**: Managing 15 different AI provider invoices.
- **Value**: Strategic consolidation roadmap.

## ✅ Product Adjustments Based on Feedback
- Added the **"Efficiency Score"** to satisfy the benchmarking desire.
- Prioritized **"Redundancy Detection"** in the engine logic.
- Implemented **"Annual Waste"** projection to make the financial impact more visceral.
